Why feed raw

A species-appropriate diet, and a pet that shows it

Do you have doubts about what to feed your pet? Every member of your family — even the furry ones — deserves nourishing, wholesome, natural food. We stock our shelves with only the finest raw pet food, made by respected suppliers, and our team will guide you to the best choice for your furry pal.

Dogs and cats are built to thrive on fresh, unprocessed food. Raw diets keep the enzymes and nutrients that heavy processing removes — and leave out the fillers, preservatives and excess carbohydrates.

New to raw?

Switching is simpler than you think

  1. Start hungry

    A half-day or full-day fast before the first raw meal makes sure your pet comes to the bowl with a good appetite.

  2. Go small

    Offer a smaller portion at first and watch how they handle the fresh food.

  3. Swap gradually

    Replace a little more of the old food with raw at each meal, adjusting the pace to your pet’s digestion.
