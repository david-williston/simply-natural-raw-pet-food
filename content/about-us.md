Simply Natural Raw Pet Food offers a wide selection of **frozen raw diets made from free-range, non-medicated meats and veggies**. Alongside them you'll find all-natural dehydrated and canned foods, raw meaty bones, a small selection of grain-free kibble, supplements, essential fatty acids, treats, toys and accessories.

## Trusted in Kerrisdale for over a decade

We've built our reputation on two things: high-quality products and staff who really know them. That's why pet professionals across the Lower Mainland send their clients to us, including:

- Trainers and K9 police handlers
- Breeders
- Rescue organizations
- Holistic veterinarians

## What you can expect

**Honest advice.** Every pet is different. Tell us about yours and we'll help you find a diet that suits their age, health and appetite — and your budget and routine.

**Food we'd feed our own.** We stock our shelves and freezers only with products from suppliers we respect.

**Help beyond the bowl.** We build [custom meal plans](/services/#meal-plans) for pets with health conditions, run [Puppy Kindergarten](/puppy-kindergarten/) classes and [deliver](/deliveries/) across Vancouver.
