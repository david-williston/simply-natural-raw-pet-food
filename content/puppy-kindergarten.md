Our Puppy Kindergarten teaches you the foundational skills for raising a puppy, using positive reinforcement and motivational techniques.

## At a glance

- **Who:** puppies 9 to 24 weeks of age, and their people
- **Format:** a one-hour class each week, for 6 weeks
- **Included:** a valuable, essential handler's bag
- **Registration:** pre-registration is required

## What you'll cover

- Basic obedience
- Tricks
- Bite inhibition
- Calming techniques
- Leash manners
- Housetraining
- …and plenty of puppy fun, with pop quizzes and prizes throughout
