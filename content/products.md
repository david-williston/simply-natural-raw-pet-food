### Avoiding raw food for cats: key considerations {#avoiding-raw}

Feeding cats a balanced diet is essential for their overall health. Some pet owners choose raw diets for their cats, while others prefer commercial or cooked alternatives. If you're considering avoiding raw food for your cat, it's important to understand the potential risks and the alternative feeding options available.

For those who wish to avoid raw food, there are several safe and nutritionally complete alternatives:

- **Commercial dry and wet cat food.** High-quality commercial cat food is formulated to meet cats' dietary needs, and undergoes rigorous testing to ensure proper nutrient balance.
- **Gently cooked diets.** Some owners prefer lightly cooked meals as an alternative to raw feeding. Properly cooked meals eliminate bacteria while maintaining nutritional value.
- **Veterinary-approved diets.** Prescription and specialty diets, available through veterinary clinics, address specific health conditions and nutritional requirements.
